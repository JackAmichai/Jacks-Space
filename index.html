How I Approach System Design

1

Understand & Clarify

Ask the right questions. What's the actual problem? Who are the users? What does success look like? I never jump to solutions before understanding constraints.

2

High-Level Architecture

Sketch the major components. APIs, databases, services, integrations. I focus on data flow first — how does information move through the system?

3

Deep Dive & Tradeoffs

Every decision has tradeoffs. Consistency vs availability? Build vs buy? I articulate options clearly and recommend based on business priorities.

4

Scale & Edge Cases

What happens at 10x load? 100x? I think about caching, sharding, async processing. And I always ask: "What could go wrong?" before it does.
